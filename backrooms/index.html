Backrooms

You noclipped out of the site. Under it there is only wireframe: rooms that go on, a drop ceiling, a hum at 60 Hz, notes on the walls in a hand you half recognise, and something tall that walks when you are not looking. Each level is grown from a seed, so it is the same when you fall back into it, and the next is bigger, lower, and a semitone down. Whoever it finds leaves a ghost, their last thirty seconds, walking the same halls for whoever comes next. Find the way down. There is no bottom.

leave your ghost

The drawing is the kind the arcades did before they had pixels to spare: every wall, floor tile and ceiling tile is a four-sided figure, filled black and outlined in phosphor, painted from the back of the room to the front, which is how a wireframe keeps what is behind a wall behind it. No WebGL, no library; one script on a plain canvas. The levels are rooms dropped on a grid until they overlap, joined by corridors, with pillars where there is room for them and the unreachable parts filled back in; the way down is wherever is farthest from where you fell. It only moves while you are not looking, which is a rule the elves insisted on. If you have been to the bazaar, the place is in colour.

When it finds you, your last thirty seconds stay behind: a pale figure that walks them again, and again, and is found where you were found, on that seed and that level, for you and for whoever comes after. Seeds come from a dozen, so the floors are shared. A ghost lives in your browser until you leave it, which opens an issue on the repo with the half minute in a code block; a workflow reads the open ones into the backrooms for everyone (tools/ghosts.py), and closing the issue lays it to rest. They cannot see you. It cannot see them.

Two more things, for when it stands in the one corridor you need. f is static: for four seconds every one of them is noise, standing where it stood, and you can walk straight through; then it is most of a minute before the trick works again, and it does not know what happened either. ` is root: a session above the floor, the whole level from overhead with every recording of it walking at once, yours and everyone's who sent theirs in, and tab climbs into any one of them to watch their last thirty seconds from where they stood. There is no server under this site, so nobody can be watched as they play; the recordings are the next thing to it, and all of them at once is the superuser's view. Without the root token, which is kept in rickbot's dungeon under /dev, a session lasts twenty seconds.
