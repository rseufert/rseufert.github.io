Backrooms

You noclipped out of the site. Under it there is only wireframe: rooms that go on, a drop ceiling, a hum at 60 Hz, notes on the walls in a hand you half recognise, and something tall that walks when you are not looking. Each level is grown from a seed, so it is the same when you fall back into it, and the next is bigger, lower, and a semitone down. Whoever it finds leaves a ghost, their last thirty seconds, walking the same halls for whoever comes next. Find the way down. There is no bottom.

kept in this browser, for the city and the backrooms both.

w a s d or arrows walk · mouse looks · shift runs · f is static · c is chalk · ` is root · esc pauses · on a phone, left thumb walks, right thumb looks, and the corner button pauses

leave your ghost post your descent

The drawing is the kind the arcades did before they had pixels to spare: every wall, floor tile and ceiling tile is a four-sided figure, filled black and outlined in phosphor, painted from the back of the room to the front, which is how a wireframe keeps what is behind a wall behind it. No WebGL, no library; one script on a plain canvas. The levels are rooms dropped on a grid until they overlap, joined by corridors, with pillars where there is room for them and the unreachable parts filled back in; the way down is wherever is farthest from where you fell. It only moves while you are not looking, which is a rule the elves insisted on. If you have been to the bazaar, the place is in colour.

When it finds you, your last thirty seconds stay behind: a pale figure that walks them again, and again, and is found where you were found, on that seed and that level, for you and for whoever comes after. Seeds come from a dozen, so the floors are shared. A ghost lives in your browser until you leave it, which opens an issue on the repo with the half minute in a code block; a workflow reads the open ones into the backrooms for everyone (tools/ghosts.py), and closing the issue lays it to rest. They cannot see you. It cannot see them.

Three more things, for when it stands in the one corridor you need. c is chalk: a circle on the floor round where you stand, which it will not cross, for twenty seconds, three pieces a level; the grimoires' rule, as a cooldown, and it only works if you draw it before it reaches you and stay inside. f is static: for four seconds every one of them is noise, standing where it stood, and you can walk straight through; then it is most of a minute before the trick works again, and it does not know what happened either. ` is root: a session above the floor, the whole level from overhead with every recording of it walking at once, yours and everyone's who sent theirs in, and tab climbs into any one of them to watch their last thirty seconds from where they stood. There is no server under this site, so nobody can be watched as they play; the recordings are the next thing to it, and all of them at once is the superuser's view. Without the root token, which is kept in rickbot's dungeon under /dev, a session lasts twenty seconds, it will not open again for a minute, and the map does not show the way down until you have seen it yourself.

Running is a choice now. A run lasts eight seconds of breath, and it hears you: for a moment after, whatever is near comes nearly as fast. From level 4 some of them sprint, faster than you down a straight hall and slow at every corner. Watched, it is only a body, and in a corridor you can edge past it against the wall; stare long enough and it looks away first and goes. Chalk drawn at its feet sends it off too, and a circle that has worn off leaves a faint ring, a trail in the fog. It finds you three times on one level and it takes you back up a level. A draft along the floor drifts toward the way down, and the hum beats faster as you get near.

Further down the levels keep growing and stay a maze, and every fourth one from level 7 is different: the lights out but for the way down, one long corridor, or a way down that will not stay put. The clock keeps your best time on each level, and a descent is timed from the top of level 0 to wherever you get; post it, the way a golf card is posted, and the deepest on each seed are shown on the way in.
