Mock SAP, EDI, bank and e-invoicing systems, for testing integrations without a license, a VPN or a trading partner. Also apps for the Flipper Zero, a dashboard of Portland and the sky, a blog and some music.

Projects

Mock enterprise systems for integration testing. Point your code at them and develop, test and demo without a license, a VPN tunnel, or a support ticket.

Using them together: Testing an SAP-to-EDI integration without SAP or a trading partner. On the failures you can't ask a partner to perform: three EDI behaviours you can't rehearse.

The films

The films are drawn by mock-films from the messages of a real run against a mock. The mocks have no screens of their own; these are the conversations they had. acme-treasury draws its own, from a real week against the mocks.

One purchase, all three mocks

A purchase order in mock-sap goes to mock-edi's supplier as an 850, comes back confirmed, shipped and invoiced, is posted into SAP as money owed, paid through mock-bank, cleared by the bank's statement, and finally advised to the supplier as an 820. Each pair of mocks was green while the chain between them was broken.

mock-sap

A black-box mock SAP endpoint: Gateway OData V2 and V4, BAPI/RFC over JSON and SOAP, and IDocs in XML and flat-file form, all backed by SQLite.

mock-edi

A mock EDI trading partner. Not an EDI library or an AS2 server, but the thing on the other end: send it an 850 and it answers with a 997, an 855, an 856 and an 810.

GitHub · PyPI

mock-bank

A mock bank that speaks ISO 20022 and NACHA, over HTTP and over a drop folder, so you can test a payment integration without a bank or a test IBAN.

GitHub · PyPI

mock-einvoice

A mock e-invoicing partner: UBL invoices and credit notes held to every published rule of EN 16931, Peppol BIS Billing and XRechnung, and the Invoice Response that answers them.

City

City overworld
