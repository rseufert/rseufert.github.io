Projects

Mock enterprise systems for integration testing. Point your code at them and develop, test and demo without a license, a VPN tunnel, or a support ticket.

Using them together: Testing an SAP-to-EDI integration without SAP or a trading partner. On the failures you can't ask a partner to perform: three EDI behaviours you can't rehearse.

The films are drawn by mock-films from the messages of a real run against each mock. The mocks have no screens of their own; these are the conversations they had. acme-treasury draws its own, from a real week against the mocks.

mock-sap

A black-box mock SAP endpoint. It speaks the shapes, not the business logic: SAP Gateway OData V2 and V4 services, BAPI/RFC calls over JSON and SOAP, and IDocs in XML and flat-file form, all backed by SQLite.

pip install mock-sap
mock-sap --port 8000
curl "http://127.0.0.1:8000/sap/opu/odata/sap/API_SALES_ORDER_SRV/A_SalesOrder?\$top=1&\$format=json"

mock-edi

A mock EDI trading partner. Not an EDI library or an AS2 server, but the thing on the other end. Send it an 850 and it answers with a 997, an 855, an 856 and an 810. Send it EDIFACT ORDERS and you get CONTRL, ORDRSP, DESADV and INVOIC.

pip install mock-edi
mock-edi --port 8080
curl -X POST --data-binary @order.edi http://127.0.0.1:8080/edi

mock-bank

A mock bank that speaks ISO 20022 and NACHA, over HTTP and over a drop folder, so you can test a payment integration without a bank, a test IBAN, or six weeks of onboarding. It is not a ledger and not a payment provider: it holds four accounts, moves balances in minor units, and refuses by name anything it does not implement.

pip install mock-bank
mock-bank --port 8090
curl -X POST --data-binary @pain001.xml http://127.0.0.1:8090/payments

One purchase, all three mocks

A purchase order in mock-sap goes to mock-edi's supplier as an 850, comes back confirmed, shipped and invoiced, is posted into SAP as money owed, paid through mock-bank, and cleared by the bank's statement. Each pair of mocks was green while the chain between them was broken.

acme-treasury

A cash forecast from what mock-sap says is owed and what mock-bank says the account holds, held to the bank's statements to the cent. When the line dips under a floor, a payment schedule says which invoice to hold, and for how long.

Worked examples

Small integrations written against the mocks, each with its tests.

  • Procure to pay all three mocks
    One purchase, carried the whole way: from a purchase order in SAP to the bank statement that clears it.
  • PO bridge mock-sap + mock-edi
    Reads a purchase order from SAP, sends it to the supplier as an X12 850, and posts the supplier's 855 back as an ORDRSP IDoc.
  • Invoice check mock-sap + mock-edi
    Posts a supplier's 810 invoice into SAP only if it passes a three-way match against the purchase order and the ship notice.
  • Pay invoices mock-edi + mock-bank
    Pays a supplier's EDIFACT INVOICs in a pain.001, then works out from what the bank sends back which are actually paid.
  • Payment run mock-sap + mock-bank
    What SAP's F110 does: select the open items that are due, pay them in one file, and post the statement back.
