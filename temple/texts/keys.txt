THE FORTY-EIGHT KEYS, OR CALLS

The nineteen Keys of the angelic language, received by John Dee and Edward Kelley at Cracow in April and May 1584, a letter at a time and backwards, and first printed in Meric Casaubon's A True & Faithful Relation of What Passed for Many Yeers Between Dr. John Dee and Some Spirits (London, 1659). The English is the angels' own, as Casaubon printed it; the angelic text is given in the spelled-out form in which the Equinox reprinted the Keys in 1912 (Liber LXXXIV vel Chanokh), from which this copy is taken, the ritual matter and the editor's notes left out. The eighteen Keys are followed by the Key of the Thirty Aethyrs, which is one Key said thirty times with the name of the Aethyr changed, and the names of the thirty. Public domain. Kept here as a plain file by rickseufert.com; the alphabet the Keys were written in is drawn at /temple/enochian/.
