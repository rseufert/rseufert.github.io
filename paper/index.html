    The puzzle

    The Daily Seed is set each midnight from the date, the way the city is laid out and the tune is written: the same number, so the same paper all day and a different one tomorrow. The match is played in the arena's own engine when the page opens, from the day's seed, so the result is the same for everyone. The river is the dashboard's USGS gauge, live. The sky is the city's, the hours are the old unequal ones cut from its sunrise and sunset, and the planets are placed from their orbital elements to about a degree, which is enough to know a sign. The ghosts are the backrooms' record. The quotation is a real paragraph from the temple's library. The geomancy is cast from the seed and judged by the old rules. The augury is read from the dashboard's live feeds, so it changes when the world does. The puzzle has a new key every day; yesterday's answer is at the bottom of it. Letters may be edited for length, and are. One script.
