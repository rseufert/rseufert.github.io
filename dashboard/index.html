dashboard

Three panels of public data, drawn when the page loads. Your browser asks each source's own API for the numbers, so they are as fresh as the source: the Treasury publishes once a business day, the bikes, the rivers and the earthquakes every few minutes. The asteroid and fireball figures come from NASA once a night; nothing else is stored here, and every chart has a table under it.
